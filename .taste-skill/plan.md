# CarCy storefront redesign

Kind: product / other. Ready-made and personalized 3D-printed goods for shoppers.
Deal: product-stage hero; Rethink Sans; owner orange #FF6500, white #FFFFFF,
surface #F3F3F0, ink #161616; centered navigation; brand-band personalization;
wordmark close. Existing Next.js, Tailwind, shadcn, and Framer Motion remain the implementation.

- Hero: product category and Facebook inquiry; live organizer on an open white stage; 1 screen.
- Collection: five explicitly illustrative concept collections; 2 desktop screens, grows with readable mobile cards.
- Personalization: name/color inquiry steps and interactive organizer color views; orange brand band; 1 desktop screen, 2 mobile screens.
- Story: maker description and supplied original logo; 1 screen.
- Close/footer: contact action and large wordmark; 1 screen.

Owner-authorized exceptions to generic Taste defaults: animation-heavy Framer Motion,
real Facebook destination, existing illustrative concept assets, honest concept/price
qualifiers, and accessible product-detail dialogs. No fabricated products, prices,
reviews, photography, availability, ordering backend, or contact details.
